Background & Philosophy

I’m Nandu, a creator who loves mixing design, motion and code. I started by making small greetings and animations for friends and family, and it grew into a passion for building complete experiences.

I believe good work feels simple on the surface and careful underneath: clear stories, smooth motion, and details that make people smile.

“Make it beautiful, make it work, make someone’s day.”
KNOWLEDGE FOCUS
  • Web design and animation
  • Video and motion graphics
  • Creative coding
CORE PRINCIPLES
  • Start with the feeling
  • Ship small, polish often
  • Respect the viewer’s time
